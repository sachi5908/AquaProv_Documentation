Introduction

AquaProv brings groundwater chemistry, statistics, mapping and uncertainty assessment into one desktop workspace. Learn why we developed it, explore its seven modules and find the software citation for your work.

What is AquaProv?#

AquaProv is a Python desktop application built with PySide6 and Matplotlib. Import a laboratory table, explore it across seven modules, and preserve the dataset and analysis settings in a portable .aquaprov project. Numerical results and methods can be exported alongside each supported analytical figure.

Motivation behind AquaProv#

Groundwater studies often involve preparing the same laboratory table for hydrochemical diagrams, statistical analyses, maps and water-quality indices. Moving between separate spreadsheets, scripts and plotting tools makes it harder to keep sample selections, units and analysis settings consistent.

We developed AquaProv to bring these steps into one accessible desktop workspace for students, researchers and practising hydrogeologists. A shared dataset connects water chemistry, statistics, spatial patterns, water-quality assessment and mineral saturation. Users can explore these relationships through the graphical interface, while Python interfaces support scripted analyses.

A second motivation is to make the evidence behind a result easier to review. Measurements near a classification boundary or screening threshold can lead to different conclusions when uncertainty or analysis choices change. AquaProv provides measurement-quality checks and uncertainty tools, and keeps available numerical results, settings and method details alongside figures. Saved projects help users revisit their work and report how a result was produced.

Seven modules, one dataset#

ModuleWhat you can do
Groundwater & contamination14 geochemical and irrigation views, from Piper composition to SAR and Wilcox classification.
Statistics12 views for correlation, PCA, Ward clustering, distributions, observed time series, and bootstrap intervals.
Maps, kriging & contours4 views for locations, kriging predictions, kriging variance, and IDW; study boundaries and map layout controls.
3D visualization4 views: XYZ scatter, linear XYZ surface, PCA sample scores, and component loadings.
Assessment & uncertainty11 views for measurement quality, conditional uncertainty, screening, compositional robustness, spatial validation, and repeated wells.
WQI & DWQI26 views: WQI and DWQI distributions, compliance, campaigns and spatial comparisons with explicit standards.
Mineral SI8 views: PHREEQC activity-based SI, imported results, mineral comparisons and spatial SI maps.

What the software preserves#

  • Projects embed the current dataset and store the settings of visited plots. Your original CSV or workbook is not rewritten.
  • Each module uses the shared dataset; each plot keeps its own method, styling, camera, and export settings.
  • Calculation outputs retain warnings, exclusions, and methods where the analysis supports them.

Scientific scope#

AquaProv visualizes and analyzes supplied measurements. Hydrochemical facies, correlations, and clusters support interpretation; they do not independently identify contamination sources, demonstrate potability, or replace a hydrogeological conceptual model. The current Assessment module includes a limited, versioned chemical screening profile, described separately from geochemical diagrams.

How to cite AquaProv#

When using AquaProv for analyses or figures in a paper, thesis or report, cite the software as follows:

Mahanta, S. N., & Phukan, B. AquaProv (Version 1.0) [Computer software]. https://github.com/sachi5908/AquaProv

In your methods, report the AquaProv version, source dataset, selected samples, units and analysis settings used. For this edition, identify the software as AquaProv V. 1.0.
