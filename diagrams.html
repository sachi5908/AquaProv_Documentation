Before you plot#

The Yang worked data are attributed to their associated source publication (Yang et al., 2020). Hydrochemical diagrams support exploratory comparison of water composition, with interpretation depending on the selected variables and graphical conventions (Güler et al., 2002; Yang et al., 2022).

  1. Confirm the required fields and measurement units.
  2. Review charge balance and missing data where the chemistry permits it.
  3. Decide whether relative composition, absolute concentration, or a process hypothesis is your question.
  4. Generate the view, inspect exclusions, and export the settings with the result.

What the example figures show#

Examples include all 2,124 Moreno Merino records, the 19-site Liu June 2003 campaign, the 29-well Yang dry 2005 campaign, and the 47 Sukinda records. Stiff and Chernoff show source sample 1. Figure captions identify input units, subsets, and limitations.
