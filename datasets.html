All seven original data files from data/ are copied into this site’s local assets/data/ folder. Four distinct datasets are represented; CSV and Excel pairs are alternate exports, not additional samples. The literature tables have bare chemistry headers: verify their units from the primary source before quantitative reuse. Row counts below were read from the supplied files.

Sukinda · CGWB-labelled workbook#

47 rows · worksheet Selected_47_Samples. Major ions, pH, EC, TDS, coordinates, and selected trace metals.

Before analyzing

Data were taken from the 2025 CGWB aquifer management plan by Sahoo et al. Trace metals in the supplied workbook are labelled ppb. Cr(VI) and total Cr are distinct fields.

Source: Sahoo et al. (2025).

Moreno Merino et al. · 2021#

2,124 rows · worksheet Data. Complete major-ion chemistry for dense composition displays.

Before analyzing

All eight major-ion fields are present; CO3 is zero throughout. The bare headers do not state units; verify them before quantitative use. No EC, TDS, pH, or coordinates are supplied. The CSV adds appearance columns. The filename contains a space before _et_al.

Related source: Moreno Merino et al. (2021).

Download Excel ↓Download CSV ↓

Liu et al. · 2021#

378 rows · worksheet Data. Repeated records with ShortID, SY (year), SM (month), longitude/latitude, and chemistry.

Before analyzing

CO3 and measured EC are absent. Missing values occur. The CSV has an extra empty Unnamed: 22 column. Retain sampling time and well identity; rows at the same site are not independent locations.

Related source: Liu et al. (2021).

Download Excel ↓Download CSV ↓

Yang et al. · 2020#

1,184 rows · worksheet Data. Repeated wells, sampling year and season, X/Y, major ions, and selected minor constituents.

Before analyzing

Text qualifiers such as n.d. occur. CO3, EC, TDS, and a source CRS declaration are absent. Do not infer the coordinate system from the large X/Y numbers or fabricate a day/month from a season.

Related source: Yang et al. (2020).

Prepared tutorial copy#

sukinda_tutorial.csv contains the same 47 source rows with normalized headers and added plotting appearance fields. Source values are unchanged; Sample is stored as text. The original workbook remains available above. Group colors use the source Label values; the documentation does not expand the abbreviations into unverified well-type definitions.

Provenance and interpretation#

The supplied WQChartPy paper identifies the Yang, Moreno Merino, and Liu datasets as examples from earlier publications. Filename associations and that statement support the citations here; they do not establish that every local row exactly reproduces a paper’s published analysis.

The live explorer below reads only the supplied Sukinda example table. It does not send measurements to a server.

WQI, DWQI & mineral saturation#

The WQI & DWQI and Mineral SI modules share the imported dataset and the standard figure/export tools. Each guide explains inputs, methods, assumptions and all of its plotting views.

25 views

Water quality indices

Arithmetic and CCME methods, independent DWQI settings, standards, compliance and campaign maps.

8 views

PHREEQC mineral saturation

Automatic chemistry mapping, activity-based SI, mineral distributions and spatial comparisons.

Sukinda index input and Liu 2015 campaign input are prepared directly from the supplied data. Preparation details are described in each plot guide. The original study-area KML is also included.
