How to compare fairly#

Each pair below was generated through the current desktop controls. The prepared input stays fixed within a pair; the named choice changes. Inspect the control panel as well as the output. Use distinct filenames for alternatives and record which option was chosen before interpreting the final study.

Group symbols by the supplied well type#

Where to change itBeforeAfter
Settings → Sample appearance → Group samples by(single group)Label

The same 47 chemical compositions are displayed. Group colors and symbols help identify the supplied well-type labels; they do not establish hydrochemical sources.

Where to change itBeforeAfter

The selected measurements and samples are held fixed. Scaling changes variable weights, score geometry and variance percentages. Choose it from the scientific question, not the most attractive separation.

Compare sampled support with full-boundary extrapolation#

Where to change itBeforeAfter
Data → Study-area boundary → Fill entire boundary (extrapolate)UncheckedChecked

The boundary and IDW power remain fixed. Full-boundary coverage adds predictions outside the sample convex hull; those cells are explicitly flagged in the exported grid. A polygon is not a groundwater-flow boundary condition.
