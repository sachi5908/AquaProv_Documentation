Time series & bootstrap

Keep observed temporal patterns separate from sampling uncertainty and measurement-error assumptions.

Mathematics and scientific background #

Follow the calculation from inputs to plotted values. The equations and conventions below follow the AquaProv Python implementation. Read them alongside this guide’s worked data, settings and exported results.

Observed time series: ordering rather than modellingIndependent-row percentile bootstrap

Observed time series: ordering rather than modelling

Time plots display observed values in temporal order (Cleveland, 1993). Each plotted point is a supplied pair (tᵢ, vᵢ). AquaProv parses the explicitly selected date format, retains complete date/measurement pairs according to the chosen policy, and sorts them stably by site/group and time. It draws straight segments between successive observations:

Displayed segment: v(t) = vᵢ + (vᵢ₊₁ − vᵢ)(t − tᵢ)/(tᵢ₊₁ − tᵢ), for distinct adjacent times

This equation describes the connecting line only; the software does not produce estimated measurements between dates. Duplicate dates are shown individually without aggregation. Choose a site column so different wells are not connected as one trajectory. Gaps in sampling, changing well coverage and seasonal timing affect interpretation; no trend significance, smoothing, forecast or causal model is inferred.

Implementation: aquaprovpy/exploration.py (time_series, plot).

Independent-row percentile bootstrap

For n retained measurements, AquaProv draws B replacement samples of size n. It computes the selected mean or median θ*b from each sample. The observed estimate θ̂ is calculated on the original retained data (Efron & Tibshirani, 1993):

Confidence interval = [Qα/2(θ*), Q1−α/2(θ*)]
α = 1 − confidence/100
Bootstrap SE = sample SD of θ*₁,…,θ*B

The histogram contains the B statistic estimates, not the original measurements. At 95% confidence the interval uses the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles (NumPy linear quantiles). Defaults are 2,000 resamples and seed 42; the accepted range is 200–20,000 with at least three complete observations. The seed makes a given configuration reproducible, not scientifically certain.

The interval concerns sampling variation of a mean or median under independent, representative rows. It is neither a prediction interval for a new measurement nor propagation of laboratory SD. Repeated observations at wells require an appropriate grouped design such as the well-bootstrap assessment. Constant observations produce a degenerate interval; small samples may give poor coverage.

Implementation: aquaprovpy/exploration.py (bootstrap, plot).

Reproduce in the desktop

Open Statistics and choose the intended view:

  • Measured series · source rows: multiple measurements against source-row positions, including explicit zero and threshold controls.
  • Time series · observed measurements: a measured quantity against parsed dates, grouped by site.
  • Uncertainty · bootstrap interval: an independent-row sampling bootstrap.

Observed time series#

Choose a measurement, date column, explicit date-string format, and optional site/group column. Real Excel datetime values are accepted. Numeric date codes are rejected as ambiguous in this workflow. Values are sorted stably within each group. Duplicate timestamps stay as individual observations and are reported.

Connecting lines are visual guides. They are not interpolation models, forecasts, or significance tests. Export the used dates, measurements, exclusions, and date format.

Use the repeated-observation datasets#

The Liu data has year SY and month SM. A display date built as the first day of each month is a documented plotting convention, not a known sampling day. The Yang table gives year and season; a season cannot be silently turned into an exact date. Use the original year/season structure for descriptive displays or the Assessment well workflow.

Independent-row bootstrap#

The Statistics bootstrap view estimates mean or median uncertainty by resampling independent rows with replacement. Select the confidence level, number of resamples, and random seed. Intervals use the percentile method with NumPy linear quantiles. The estimate, resample distribution, standard error, bounds, and source values are exported.

Constant data produce a degenerate interval and a warning. Small sample sizes or low resample counts need particular scrutiny. The implementation caps total draws to control work. Reference: Efron and Tibshirani (1993).

Choose the correct resampling unit#

Rows may not be independent

Repeated samples from the same well, seasonal observations, spatially correlated sites, and weighted survey data require a design-aware method. An ordinary row bootstrap does not represent laboratory error or a future-observation prediction interval.

Assessment’s whole-well bootstrap weights well summaries equally and distinguishes the equal-well mean from the pooled measurement mean. Its within-well trends are descriptive; see the assessment guide.
